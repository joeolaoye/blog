I build the payments core for fintech founders — the ledger, reconciliation, payout rails, and the compliance plumbing nobody demos but everybody depends on. Two things I'm building right now, in the open:

**conchpay** — a turnkey payments platform: merchant acquisition, collections, and disbursement, with dual bank rails, a USDC settlement path, and KYT/KYB built in. It's at the design stage, and it's built to be sold and run by the operator who buys it.

**borderless** — stablecoin cross-border settlement. It's at the PRD stage.

I write up the real decisions as I make them: the tradeoffs, the failure modes, the things I get wrong the first time. No launch hype — just the work, while it's still being built.
